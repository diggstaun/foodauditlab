What the Stress Test covers

Each area maps to specific requirements in IFS Food, BRCGS, FSSC 22000, and EU Regulation 178/2002.

A
Lot Traceability
Incoming materials, semi-finished goods, finished products. One-up / one-down traceability. Lot coding logic and consistency.
7 questions
B
Mass Balance
Per-batch measurement, variance recording, lot-linked waste and rework, packaging material traceability. The area most commonly misunderstood.
5 questions
C
Documentation & Records
Real-time completion, retrieval speed, audit trail integrity. Can you pull a full trace in under 4 hours?
5 questions
D
System & Controls
ERP vs paper, error-prevention controls, mock recall frequency and variation, formal recall procedure and recall team readiness.
10 questions

Non-Conforming Product
Segregation, hold status, release authorisation, documentation of disposition decisions. A common source of major non-conformities.
4 questions

Approved supplier list, manufacturer visibility through distributors, risk-based supplier monitoring and re-evaluation.

Common questions

FAQ

Do I need any special software?
No. The tool is an Excel file (.xlsx) that works in Microsoft Excel (2016 or later) and Google Sheets. No plugins, no subscriptions, no additional software required.
How long does it take to complete?
Most users complete the questionnaire in 45–90 minutes. If you need to gather information from production records or colleagues first, allow a few hours. The dashboard and action log populate instantly as you answer.
Which certification standards does it cover?
The tool is mapped to IFS Food v8, BRCGS Food Safety, FSSC 22000 v6, ISO 22000:2018, and EU Regulation 178/2002 (General Food Law). Each question references the relevant standard requirements.
Is this a guarantee of audit success?
No tool can guarantee audit outcomes. The Stress Test gives you an honest, structured view of where your system sits — so you can address gaps before the auditor finds them. It is a preparation tool, not a certification service.
Can I use it for multiple sites or share it with colleagues?
Each purchase covers a single-user licence. For multi-site use or team licences, contact us at info@foodauditlab.com and we'll work out the right arrangement.
Who built this tool?
FoodAuditLab tools are built by a practising food safety auditor with experience across GFSI-recognised certification schemes. The questions and red flags come directly from real audit findings — not from reading standards alone.
